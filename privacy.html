Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Samvaad ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Samvaad app and related services. We keep this simple: we collect what we need to run the product, and nothing we can't justify.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — such as your username, email address, and password (stored encrypted).
  • Debate activity — the topics you join, arguments you post, matches, and rankings.
  • Device and usage data — app version, device type, and basic analytics that help us fix bugs and improve the experience.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and match you with debate partners.
  • To operate features like rankings, notifications, and curated topics.
  • To keep Samvaad safe by detecting abuse and enforcing our Terms.
  • To understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

3. What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show your email or private account details to other users. Your debates may be visible to others as part of the core experience.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run Samvaad (for example, hosting and analytics), and only as needed. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email hello@samvaad.app.

7. Children's privacy

Samvaad is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email.

9. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at hello@samvaad.app.
